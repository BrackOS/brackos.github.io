The people behind Brack OS

About the team.

Brack OS is built by a small team working on the project in their spare time.

Brent

Brent is a co-founder and the CEO of Brack OS.

He does the main kernel development work, including writing assembly and C, debugging, and working on the core system.

He does his work on a gaming PC dual-booting Windows 11 and Linux.

Jack

Jack is a co-founder, the COO, and CFO of Brack OS.

He works on the website, GitHub management, code review, and testing.

He does his work on a Raspberry Pi 5 running Linux.

Noah

Noah is a member of the Brack OS team who mainly works on C and related development.

His involvement is somewhat limited because of his other projects and commitments.

He does his work on a MacBook (Running Tahoe,ick :).

Jayden

Jayden is the Chief Marketing Officer of Brack OS.

He works on marketing and helping get the project in front of people who might be interested in it.

He does his work on a Raspberry Pi 4.
