# What we're working on now and what we plan to do soon
### Progress is measured on a scale of 1-10, 1 being just started, 5 being working but buggy and unreleasable, and 10 pretty much being "It'll be released within a couple days"
### Priority level carries no guarantees for release dates or anything of the sort. 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
### Note that this page will have formatting issues and rough edges, but that is on purpose to make it to where we can update it quickly whenever needed.



#### Native Filesystem named BRKS or BRKFS
**Progress: 4**
**Priority: 5**

A native filesystem to give us more than FAT32 can give. 
Jack is developing a driver for Linux so it will be usable for more than just BrackOS.
The driver will be released later, probably not when the Filesystem launches
**Primary dev(s)** Jack
