Donor Names

Want your name on the page?

If you donate cryptocurrency and would like your name listed on this page, send an email to brackosteam@proton.me with:

  • The name you'd like us to display
  • The amount you sent
  • The cryptocurrency you sent
  • The address you sent it from

Any cryptocurrency donation received without a corresponding email will be regarded as anonymous and will not be publicly attributed to a name.

Donor Categories

How the $15 cutoff works.

The $15 threshold is based on the USD value of a contribution at the time it is received. Cryptocurrency contributions are therefore categorized according to their USD value when the donation reaches us.

The donor lists are a way of recognizing supporters, not a measure of how much their support matters.

Where Support Goes

Supporting an open-source OS.

Financial support can help with the practical costs of developing BrackOS, including development hardware, testing, storage, infrastructure, and other project expenses.

BrackOS remains open source, and donations help us continue developing the project while keeping the software and its source available to everyone.

Prefer to Contribute?

You can help without donating.

Code, testing, documentation, ideas, and other contributions are all valuable to BrackOS.
