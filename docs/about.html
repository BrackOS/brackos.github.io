The Team

Built by a small team.

BrackOS is an open-source operating system project developed by a three-person team. We each have different responsibilities, interests, and areas of expertise, but we're working toward the same goal: building an operating system from the ground up.

CEO · CFO · Co-Founder

Jack

Jack handles the company's executive and financial responsibilities and develops and maintains the BrackOS website.

Outside of BrackOS, Jack is interested in radio technology and amateur radio. He is a licensed ham radio operator and MeshCore operator, with a particular interest in experimenting with radio equipment and decentralized communications.

Focus: Web development, organization, and project direction.

CTO · Co-Founder

Brent

Brent leads the majority of BrackOS's technical development and is responsible for much of the work happening inside the operating system itself.

He also enjoys building automated software projects and developing games.

Focus: Kernel and systems development, automation, and software projects.

COO

Jayden

Jayden handles operations and helps coordinate meetings and work across the team. He also contributes wherever the project needs additional help.

Outside of BrackOS, Jayden is a Raspberry Pi enthusiast and enjoys experimenting with modifications and applications of large language models.

Focus: Operations, coordination, experimentation, and general project support.
