# What we're working on now and what we plan to do soon ### Progress is measured on a scale of 1-10, 1 being just started, 5 being working but buggy and unreleasable, and 10 pretty much being "It'll be released within a couple days" ### Priority level carries no guarantees for release dates or anything of the sort. 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. ### Note that this page will have formatting issues and rough edges, but that is on purpose to make it to where we can update it quickly whenever needed. #### Native Filesystem named BRKS **Progress: 6** **Priority: 2** A native filesystem to give us more than FAT32 can give. Jack is developing a driver for Linux so it will be usable for more than just BrackOS. The driver will be released later, probably not when the Filesystem launches. This has been released in the source code, but will remain beta until further testing. **Primary dev(s)** Jack #### Getting a basic GUI working **Progress: 2** **Priority: 1** The title should be self-explanatory. We are limited on time so development is very slow. So much of it is just tedious work, so we have drafted a Chinese AI (Local, of course!) to help us do the boring parts. **Primary dev(s)** Jack, Jayden, Deepseek-r1:14b :) #### **VERY BASIC** Ethernet support **Progress: 3** **Priority: 4** Intel e1000 card support for BrackOS. For now this will just work in Qemu _(Although if you have an e1000 card maybe it could work on real hardware...)_ For now it is just constant polling on the PCI bus and only the ping command is functional, but... It works! Once we get DNS in place, installing apps via the ```bndl``` command will be possible by downloading app manifests from a GitHub repo. **Primary dev(s)** Jack ## Future work #### USB support **Progress: 0** **Priority: 0** One of the hardest things to do for an OS is to develop drivers, and BrackOS is no exception. This might be implemented in the form of basic usb drive and Keyboard/mouse within 2 years, but keep your expectations down **Primary dev(s)** None.
