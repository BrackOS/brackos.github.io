The team

Meet the people behind Brack OS.

Brack OS is being built by a small team working on the project in their spare time.

Co-founder · CEO

Brent R.

Brent is a co-founder and the CEO of Brack OS. He handles much of the core development work on the project.

His work includes assembly, C, debugging, and working with development tools and AI-assisted tools.

He develops on a gaming PC running Windows 11 and Linux.

Co-founder · COO · CFO

Jack C.

Jack is a co-founder, COO, and CFO of Brack OS.

He works on the website, GitHub management, code review, and testing.

He does his work on a Raspberry Pi 5 with 8 GB of RAM running Linux.

Developer

Noah H.

Noah is a newer member of the Brack OS team and primarily works with C.

His involvement is somewhat limited because of his other projects and his preparation for college.

He develops on a MacBook running macOS Tahoe.

Chief Marketing Officer

Jayden L.

Jayden is the Chief Marketing Officer of Brack OS and helps with the project's outreach and presentation.

He's currently working on a Raspberry Pi 4 with 4 GB of RAM.

There's always room for more people to join the team.
