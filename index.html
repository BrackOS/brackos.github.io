About Brack OS

Brack OS is an open-source os for (hopefully someday) all platforms. Right now, it only operates on x64 CPUs and is to buggy to release even a canary build, but hopefully soon that will change.

Devs: Brent R. Jack C. Noah H. Jayden L.

Upcoming platforms (in order of priority) x64, ARM64, i386/x86, ARM32, 16bit. Once we get all/most architectures working, we will start developing a GUI. Expect that to be after a few years and much driver troubleshooting:). Until then, thanks for reading.
