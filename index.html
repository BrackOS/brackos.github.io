The Project

Why BrackOS?

Operating systems are the software layer between hardware and the programs that run on it. They manage resources, communicate with hardware, and provide the foundations that applications depend on.

BrackOS is our attempt to understand those foundations by building them ourselves. That means working close to the processor, memory, boot process, and hardware instead of hiding those details behind an existing operating system.

It's an ongoing project, and there's plenty left to build.
