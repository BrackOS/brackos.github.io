ARM32 kernel info

The ARM32 kernel is our first priority once we get 16 bit working.

We are thinking that the Canary build for ARM32 will be out in early 2027!

We can make no guarantees, but our hope is that the stable version 0.1 will be out in summer 2027.

Once either build is out, you can get the code can instantly at our github, or you can come back here where a download button will be posted.
