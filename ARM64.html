Architecture / ARM64

ARM64

A major future target for Brack OS, opening the door to a wide range of modern ARM-based hardware.

High-priority future platform

ARM64 is important to the long-term direction of Brack OS, but development has not reached the point where a release date can be estimated.

Why ARM64 matters

ARM64 has become an important architecture across phones, tablets, single-board computers, laptops, and other modern devices.

Supporting it would significantly expand the hardware that Brack OS could eventually run on.

Current plans

Status

Planned and considered a high-priority architecture.

Release

No release estimate is currently available.

Hardware

The long-term goal is to support a variety of ARM64 devices.

Next step

Continue building the core system before expanding to additional architectures.

Stay up to date

The best place to follow ARM64 development is the Brack OS source repository.
